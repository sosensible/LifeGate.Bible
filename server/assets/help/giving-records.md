A **giving record** is who a statement goes to. It is usually a household, so
one statement covers a husband and wife who give from the same account.

Open **Givers** in the Stewardship tabs.

## It is kept apart from the directory

The name, mailing address and email on a giving record are yours to keep, not
copied from the church directory. That is deliberate: giving is how the church
addresses a tax statement, and it should not change because someone edited
their own profile.

**Link to the directory** is optional and only offers you the household's or
person's name to start from. Removing someone from the directory leaves their
giving record alone.

## Add a record

1. Press **Add giving record**.
2. Optionally link a household or a person, which fills in the name.
3. **Name on statements** — how it should read, for example "John & Mary Smith".
4. **Mailing address** and **Email**.
5. **Send statements by** — Mail or Email.
6. **Notes** — optional, for your own use.

A name alone is enough to enter gifts, which is why a Counter can add a bare
name on a Sunday. Fill in the rest before statements go out.

## Records that cannot be sent a statement

The page warns, for example, "3 giving records can't receive a statement yet".
That means the address is missing, or the record is set to email with no email
address. Open each one and complete it.

## Yearly totals

**Given in {year}** counts **closed counts only**. A total that looks low
usually means a count is still open. Close it, and the figure catches up.

The **Gifts** list inside a record shows every gift, for all years, with an
**Open count** badge on any that is not counted yet.

## Archive, not remove

Turn on **Archived** and save when someone leaves or stops giving. The record
drops out of the giver list used on Sunday, and every past gift and statement
stays exactly as it was. **Show archived** brings them back into view.

**Remove** appears only for a record with no gifts at all, for a name added by
mistake. The site refuses to remove a record that has gifts: "This giving record
has gifts, so it can only be archived." Removing happens at once, with no "are
you sure".
