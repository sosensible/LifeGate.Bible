A giving statement is the church's written record of what one giver gave in a
year, in the wording the tax rules expect. Open **Statements** in the Stewardship
tabs.

## Before you send anything

1. **Pick the year.** The year box opens on last year, except during December,
   when it opens on this year.
2. **Clear any open counts.** A warning at the top says, for example, "2 counts
   are still open for 2026", with **Go to offerings**. Gifts in an open count
   are not on a statement. Close them first, or the statements you send will be
   short.
3. **Complete the giving records** that cannot receive anything. A row marked
   "· missing details" has no address, or is set to email with no email address.
   See [Giving records](/help/giving-records).
4. **Check the wording once** in **Statement settings**. See
   [Statement settings](/help/statement-settings).

## What the list shows

One row per giving record that has gifts on closed counts that year:

- the name, and how many gifts;
- **By** — Mail or Email;
- **Total** for the year;
- **Sent** — Not sent, or Printed or Emailed with when, or Email failed with the
  reason behind the badge;
- **Changed since sent** — the total is no longer what it was when the statement
  went out, so a corrected one should go.

Loose and anonymous gifts are on nobody's statement, and never appear here.

## Check one before sending many

**Preview** opens that giver's statement as a PDF. Preview is safe: it does not
count as sending and records nothing.

## Send them

- **Print mailed statements (N)** makes one PDF of everyone who gets theirs by
  post, ready to fold into window envelopes. It includes anyone with no email
  address, whatever their setting.
- **Email statements (N)** sends each giver their own PDF. You are asked to
  confirm, and the result says how many went, for example "38 statements sent"
  or "37 sent, 1 failed" with the reason for each failure. That message stays on
  screen until you close it, so failures cannot slip past.
- The printer on a single row prints that one statement.

**Printing records the statement as sent.** That is true of the single-row
printer as well as the big button, and there is nothing to confirm it first.
The badge may take a moment, or a page refresh, to appear. A record of sending
cannot be undone or deleted, so print deliberately and use **Preview** when you
only want to look.

Sending again is allowed as often as you like; the newest delivery is the one
shown.

## What is on the statement

The church's name, address and tax number at the top; the giver's name and
address positioned for a window envelope; the line "Contributions received
January 1 through December 31" of the year; every gift with its date, method and
amount, with "Given to" shown only when something was designated; subtotals per
designation when there is more than one; the total; the fixed sentence the tax
rules require; your closing message and signature; and "Please keep this
statement for your tax records."

## Worth knowing

- The list and the totals count **closed counts only**.
- **Archived** giving records still appear here, and are included when you print
  or email in bulk. Check the list before a big send.
- **Changed since sent** compares totals only. If a designation changed but the
  total did not, nothing is flagged.
- The audit log records that statements were printed or emailed, with the year
  and the counts, for example "2026: 38 sent, 1 failed". It never records a name,
  an amount or an email address.
