Most gifts are simply given to the church. A gift given for something in
particular is **designated**.

## Undesignated is the normal case

An undesignated gift is just an amount. It goes to **Available to Fund**, which
is the money the church has to budget with. Enter the amount and nothing else.

## Designating a gift

Press **Designated gift** in the Enter a gift card, then choose what it was
given for in **Given to**.

The list is the church's budget categories, for example Missions. There is no
separate list of funds: a designated gift goes to the budget category it
belongs to, which is where that spending is already planned and tracked.

Only categories in use are offered. If the one you need is not there, the
Treasurer adds it on the Categories page first.

## A memo, when the category does not say enough

A designated line has a **Memo** box: "what it's for, if the category doesn't
say". Use it for something specific inside a category, for example "VBS snacks"
in a Youth category.

The memo stays with the gift. It is not copied to the bank deposit's budget
lines, which ministry leaders can see, so a memo is safe to be plain about what
the giver asked for.

A memo belongs only to a designated line. There is no memo on an undesignated
gift.

## One check, more than one purpose

If someone writes one check for two things, for example $100 with $60 for
missions:

1. Press **Designated gift**.
2. Enter the first line, with its category and amount.
3. Press **Add a line** and enter the next one. Up to ten lines.
4. A line can be left as **Undesignated (Available to Fund)**, so part of the
   check is designated and the rest is not.

Press **All undesignated** to collapse the lines back to one plain amount,
carrying the total.

Each line is kept as its own gift, which is why the gift list can show several
rows for one check. They all carry the same check number, method and date.

**The gift editor cannot split a gift.** To split a gift you entered as one
amount, remove it and enter it again as lines.

## Where designated money ends up

When the Treasurer links the closed count to the bank deposit, undesignated
giving goes to Available to Fund and each designated amount goes to its
category. The deposit's lines name categories only, never givers. See
[Linking the deposit](/help/linking-a-deposit).
