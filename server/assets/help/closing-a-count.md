Closing a count says the offering is counted and agreed. Only the Treasurer can
close or reopen one. Closing matters beyond tidiness: **only gifts on closed
counts appear on giving statements** and in a giver's yearly total.

## Closing

Press **Close count** at the top of the count. It stays greyed out until the
count matches the paper sheet:

- the cash you entered equals **Cash on the count sheet**;
- the checks you entered equal **Checks on the count sheet**;
- there is at least one gift.

If it will not close, the badges on the **Cash** and **Checks** cards show
which one is wrong and by how much. Fix the gifts, or correct the sheet totals
with **Edit details** if the sheet itself was mistyped.

The site refuses a close it cannot stand behind, with "The gifts don't match the
count sheet yet" or "This count has no gifts".

**Online and other gifts are not checked against anything.** There is no line
for them on the paper sheet, so nothing compares them. Take your own care with
those.

## After closing

A closed count cannot be changed. Counters no longer see it at all. The next
step is [linking the bank deposit](/help/linking-a-deposit).

## Reopening

Press **Reopen** at the top of a closed count, and say why. The reason is kept
in the count's **Notes**, on the count itself. The audit log records that the
count was reopened, but never the reason, because a reason may name a giver or
an amount.

While a count is reopened, its gifts drop off statements until it is closed
again.

**Reopen is not offered while a deposit is linked.** To correct a count that is
already deposited, work in this order:

1. **Unlink** the deposit on the count's Deposit card.
2. **Reopen** the count, and say why.
3. Fix the gifts.
4. **Close count** again.
5. **Link deposit** again.

## What cannot be done

- A count cannot be deleted or voided. A count started by mistake can be left
  with no gifts, but it stays in the list.
- Notes cannot be typed directly. The only thing written there is a reopening
  reason.
- A gift cannot be moved to another count. Remove it from one and enter it in
  the other.
