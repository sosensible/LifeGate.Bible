Nothing in giving is thrown away, so almost every mistake is fixed by editing or
by reopening. The right steps depend on whether the count is still open.

## The count is still open

Use the pencil on the gift's row for the giver, category, memo, amount, method,
check number or date. Use the bin to remove a gift entered twice. Neither asks
for confirmation.

## The count is closed but not deposited

Ask the Treasurer to **Reopen** it and say why, fix the gifts, then close it
again. See [Closing and reopening a count](/help/closing-a-count).

## The count is closed and the deposit is linked

In this order: **Unlink** the deposit, **Reopen** the count, fix the gifts,
**Close count**, then **Link deposit** again.

## The same gift was entered twice

Nothing warns you about this: a repeated giver, amount or check number is
accepted, and the same check number can be used twice. Guard against it by
letting the cash and check totals from the paper sheet be your check. A
duplicate makes the count refuse to close, because it will be over by the
duplicated amount.

## A gift went to the wrong giver

Edit the gift and choose the right giver. If it was on a statement that already
went out, the Statements page flags both records with **Changed since sent**, so
send both a corrected statement.

## A gift was put in the wrong year

The **Received on** date decides the year, not the count's date. Edit the gift's
date. Nothing stops you setting a date in any year, so check it after a change.

## The statement has already been sent

Fix the gifts first. The Statements page then shows **Changed since sent** on
that row, and you can print or email a fresh one. Sending again is allowed as
often as needed; the newest delivery is the one recorded. A delivery record
cannot be deleted, so the history shows the corrected statement as well as the
first one.

Note that **Changed since sent** only notices a different total. A correction
that leaves the total the same, such as moving a gift between categories, is not
flagged: keep your own note to re-send.

## Things that cannot be fixed this way

- **A count cannot be deleted.** One started by mistake can be left empty, but
  it stays in the list. Give it a service name like "started in error" with
  **Edit details** so it is clear later.
- **A gift cannot be moved to another count.** Remove it from the wrong count
  and enter it on the right one.
- **A gift cannot be split in the editor.** Remove it and enter it again with
  lines. See [Designated gifts](/help/designated-gifts).
