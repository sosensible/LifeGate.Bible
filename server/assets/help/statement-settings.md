These are the parts of a giving statement that are the same for everyone. Press
**Statement settings** at the top of the Statements page.

- **Church legal name** — required. It is the name at the top of the statement
  and in the email. Use the church's legal name, the one that matches its tax
  registration.
- **Mailing address** — printed under the name.
- **EIN** — the church's federal tax number, written like 12-3456789. Optional,
  but givers and their tax preparers expect it.
- **Signed by** and **Title** — for example the Treasurer's name and
  "Treasurer", printed at the end.
- **Closing message** — a sentence of thanks, printed above the signature.
- **Email subject** — for emailed statements. Left empty, the subject reads
  "Your {year} contribution statement from {church name}".

Only the Treasurer can save changes. Anyone who can see statements can read the
settings.

## The tax wording is fixed

Every statement carries this sentence, and it cannot be edited:

> No goods or services were provided in exchange for these contributions other
> than intangible religious benefits.

It is the acknowledgment the tax rules expect, so it is kept in the site's code
rather than left to be edited or deleted by mistake. If your accountant wants
different wording, that is a change to make in the code.

There is no logo on statements.

Changes are recorded in the audit log by field name, never by value.
