Every offering is entered as one **count**. You start it from the paper count
sheet, enter the gifts, and the count is finished when what you entered matches
the sheet.

Open **Offerings** from your account menu.

## Start a count

1. Press **Start a count**.
2. **Date** — the day of the offering. Today's date is filled in.
3. **Service** — optional, for example "Sunday morning". Use it when there is
   more than one offering on the same day.
4. **Cash on the count sheet** and **Checks on the count sheet** — the two
   totals from the paper sheet. Enter them before the gifts; they are what the
   count is checked against.
5. **Counted by** — optional, for example the two names on the sheet.
6. Press **Start count**. The new count opens, ready for gifts.

If you need to correct the date, service, sheet totals or names later, press
**Edit details** at the top of an open count.

## Enter a gift

Use the **Enter a gift** card on an open count.

1. **Giver** — start typing a name and pick the giving record.
   - For loose cash and anything you cannot attribute, choose **Loose /
     anonymous (not on a statement)**. Loose cash is never on a giving
     statement, because nobody can be credited for it.
   - If the giver is not on the list, type the name and choose **Add "…" as a
     new giver**. That adds the name only; the Treasurer fills in the address
     later.
2. **Method** — Cash, Check, Online, Bank transfer or Other. It stays on your
   last choice as you enter one gift after another, so all the checks can be
   entered together.
3. **Check number** — appears for checks, and is optional.
4. **Amount** — the amount of the gift.
5. Press the button, which shows what you are about to add, for example
   **Add $50.00**.

For a gift given for something in particular, see
[Designated gifts](/help/designated-gifts).

After each gift the giver, check number and amount clear, and the cursor goes
back to the giver box, so a stack of checks can be entered without using the
mouse.

### A gift received on another day

Tick **Received on a different date** and set the date. Use it for a check
written and mailed in December that you count in January: the date decides which
year's giving statement it belongs on.

There is no check on that date, so take care: nothing stops you putting a gift
in the wrong year.

## Check your work as you go

The four cards at the top of the count keep score while you enter:

- **Cash** and **Checks** each show the sheet total, and a badge saying
  **Matches** or **Off by $…**.
- **Online and other** has no sheet total to compare against.
- **Total** shows how many gifts you have entered.

The **Undesignated and designated** card below shows what the offering adds up
to, undesignated first, then each category.

## Change or remove a gift

While the count is open:

- The pencil on a gift's row opens **Edit gift**, where you can change the
  giver, category, memo, amount, method, check number and date.
- The bin removes the gift. **It is removed at once, with no "are you sure".**

Once the count is closed, nothing in it can be changed until it is reopened. See
[Closing a count](/help/closing-a-count).
