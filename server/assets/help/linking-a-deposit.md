A count records what was given. Linking tells the budget where that money went,
by pointing the count at the bank deposit. Until then the deposit sits
uncategorized in the church's accounts.

Linking needs permission to keep the budget as well as giving, which in practice
means the Treasurer.

## Link a deposit that is in the bank

1. Open the closed count. The **Deposit** card shows what to look for, for
   example "Link the bank deposit for $1,250.00".
2. Press **Link deposit**.
3. Under **From the bank** you get the deposits that could be this count:
   - exactly the count's total;
   - in a checking, savings or cash account;
   - within a week either side of the count's date;
   - not categorized yet, and not already linked to another count.
4. Press **Link** on the right row.

The deposit is then split for you: undesignated giving to **Available to Fund**,
each designated amount to its category, and the payee is set to "Offering" if it
was blank. The lines name categories, never givers, because ministry leaders can
see them.

**"No matching deposit yet"** means nothing in the accounts matches. Usually the
bank has not sent it through yet: press **Check the bank** on the Transactions
page, or wait for the next automatic check. It can also mean the deposit is a
few cents out from the count, or that it is already categorized, in which case
fix the transaction on the Transactions page first.

## Record it in a manual account instead

Use this when the money did not go to the bank, for example a cash box you keep
yourself.

In the same window, under **Or record it in a manual account**, choose the
account and the date, then press **Record deposit**. That writes the deposit
into that account for the count's total and splits it the same way.

Only accounts you keep by hand are offered. An account fed by the bank cannot
take a transaction that the bank has not sent.

## Unlink

**Unlink** on the Deposit card undoes it: the transaction goes back to one
uncategorized line. **It happens at once, with no "are you sure".** Unlink when
the wrong deposit was linked, or before reopening a count to correct it.

Both linking and unlinking are in the audit log, naming the count and the
transaction, never a giver.
